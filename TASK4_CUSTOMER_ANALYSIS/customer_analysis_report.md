# Customer Data Analysis Report

## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer data, identify purchasing behavior, segment customers into different groups, and provide business insights for marketing.

---

## Dataset Information

Dataset: Mall Customers Dataset

Total Customers: 200

Number of Features: 5

- CustomerID
- Gender
- Age
- Annual Income (k$)
- Spending Score (1-100)

---

## Data Exploration

The dataset was loaded successfully.

No duplicate records were found.

No missing values were found.

Summary statistics were generated using Pandas.

---

## Customer Segmentation

Customers were grouped into:

- Young
- Adult
- Senior

K-Means clustering was applied to classify customers based on Annual Income and Spending Score.

---

## High Value Customers

Customers having a Spending Score greater than or equal to 80 were considered high-value customers.

These customers are likely to purchase more frequently and should receive premium offers.

---

## Key Findings

- Adult customers represent the largest customer segment.
- Female customers have a slightly higher average spending score than male customers.
- High-income customers do not always spend more.
- Customer segmentation helps identify profitable customer groups.
